Robust Variance Estimation (RVE)
================================

Generated 2026-10-19 with allmeta — browser-only evidence synthesis (https://github.com/).

METHODS

Cluster-robust meta-regression for dependent effects using the Hedges-Tipton-Johnson CORR working model, CR2 bias-reduced standard errors and per-coefficient Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. Inference can be unreliable with very low degrees of freedom; inspect the coefficient-specific df and sensitivity to the working correlation.

Engine: shared/rve.js — Hedges-Tipton-Johnson 2010 correlated-effects working model with the CR2 bias-reduced small-sample correction (Tipton 2015; Pustejovsky-Tipton 2018) and per-coefficient Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, with potentially unreliable inference at very low degrees of freedom. τ² is the HTJ CORR moment estimator (not a plain DerSimonian-Laird across rows). t-quantiles via safeguarded inversion of the Student-t CDF. Reference implementation: robumeta::robu(modelweights="CORR", small=TRUE). Check numerical conditioning and coefficient-specific degrees of freedom before interpreting inference. Because the CORR weights are constant within a cluster, the within-cluster annihilator block is symmetric, so Aj is a true symmetric inverse-square-root. CR1 (a single scalar correction, anticonservative at small m) remains available via fitCORR(rows,{method:"CR1"}). The HIER working model is not yet implemented; for hierarchical effects use robumeta::robu(model="HIER") in R.
