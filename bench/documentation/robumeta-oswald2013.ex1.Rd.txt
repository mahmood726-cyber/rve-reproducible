_D_e_s_c_r_i_p_t_i_o_n:

     Data from a meta-analysis on IAT conducted by Oswald et al.,
     (2013) examining the predictive validity of the Implicit
     Association Test (IAT) and various explicit measures of bias for a
     variety of criterion measures of discrimination. Included in the
     dataset are the study-level correlations between IAT scores and
     criterion measures of neurological activity or response latency
     from the original 'oswald2013' dataset.
